Your Learning Roadmap

01

Foundation (Months 1-2)

🎯 AI & ML Fundamentals

  • What is Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • Difference between AI, ML, and Deep Learning
  • History and evolution of AI technology
  • Real-world AI applications across industries

🤖 Large Language Models (LLMs)

  • How LLMs work and their architecture
  • Popular models: GPT, BERT, Claude, LLaMA
  • Understanding transformers and attention mechanisms
  • Training vs. fine-tuning vs. prompt engineering

⚖️ GenAI Ethics & Governance

  • Responsible AI principles and frameworks
  • Bias detection and mitigation
  • Privacy and data protection considerations
  • Compliance and regulatory requirements
02

Core Skills (Months 3-4)

🐍 Python Programming

  • Python fundamentals and syntax
  • Data structures: lists, dictionaries, tuples
  • Libraries: NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib
  • Object-oriented programming basics

📊 Data Science Essentials

  • Data collection and preprocessing
  • Exploratory data analysis (EDA)
  • Statistical analysis and visualization
  • Working with datasets from various sources

☁️ Cloud & APIs

  • Introduction to AWS, Azure, and GCP
  • Working with APIs and REST services
  • API authentication and data handling
  • Deploying simple applications to cloud
03

GenAI Specialization (Months 5-8)

✏️ Prompt Engineering Mastery

  • Prompt design principles and best practices
  • Few-shot and chain-of-thought prompting
  • Temperature, top-k, top-p parameters tuning
  • Advanced prompt templates and frameworks

🔧 Model Fine-tuning & Customization

  • Transfer learning and fine-tuning concepts
  • Preparing custom datasets for training
  • Using HuggingFace and other frameworks
  • Evaluation metrics and performance optimization

🔍 RAG & Advanced Architectures

  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems
  • Vector databases and embeddings
  • Building knowledge bases and context systems
  • Combining LLMs with external data sources

🚀 Building GenAI Applications

  • LangChain and AI frameworks
  • Building chatbots and conversational AI
  • Text generation and content creation tools
  • Integrating with business applications
04

Professional Practice (Months 9+)

� Production & Deployment

  • Containerization (Docker and Kubernetes)
  • CI/CD pipelines for AI applications
  • Model versioning and registry
  • Monitoring and logging in production

⚡ Performance & Optimization

  • Model optimization and quantization
  • Latency and throughput improvements
  • Cost optimization strategies
  • Scalability and load testing

👥 Leadership & Strategy

  • Leading GenAI projects and teams
  • Stakeholder management and communication
  • AI strategy and roadmap planning
  • Building high-performing AI teams

📚 Continuous Learning

  • Following latest GenAI research papers
  • New model releases and frameworks
  • Industry conferences and workshops
  • Contributing to open-source projects
